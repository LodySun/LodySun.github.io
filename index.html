Jianlun (Lody) Sun

Neuroscience · University of Helsinki

I recently completed my MSc in Neuroscience at the University of Helsinki (2024–2026), following a BSc in Biological Sciences at ShanghaiTech University (2018–2022), and I am currently looking for a PhD position.

My work spans cognitive flexibility and narrative memory. I began with structural-MRI research on locus coeruleus integrity and set-shifting in Parkinson's disease at the CAS Institute of Neuroscience, then studied the dynamics of rule learning through behavioral modeling, hidden Markov models, EEG, and inter-subject representational similarity analysis. More recently, I developed a multivariate fMRI pipeline to investigate how earlier narrative context is reinstated when people recall later, related events.

For my PhD, I want to study narrative and event memory: how people build, update, and reconstruct relationships between events. I am particularly interested in context reinstatement, individual differences in remembering, and how narrative devices shape predictions and our understanding of what others know. Across these questions, I hope to connect computational descriptions of behavior with neural measures of how experience is organised and remembered.
